Key terms

Surface urban heat island
How much warmer the land surface is than the countryside around the city. It is a land temperature, not the temperature of the air people breathe.
Land surface temperature
The temperature of the ground, roofs and tree tops as a satellite sees it on clear days, here from Landsat images taken around 11:00 local time, December to February.
Countryside reference
Land within 20 km of the state that ESA WorldCover 2021 classes as trees, shrubs, grass, crops, wetland or mangrove. Each cell’s heat is measured against it.
NDVI
A greenness index from satellite images. A cell counts as vegetated when its median NDVI is at least 0.4.
300 m from vegetation
The straight-line distance used to split residents into those near vegetation and those farther away (about four minutes’ walk on a direct path).
Built-up cell
A cell that ESA WorldCover 2021 classes as built-up. The main comparison is between built-up cells only. Land cover comes from the single 10 m label at each cell centre, so it is only roughly held fixed.
WorldPop
A modelled map of where people live, at 100 m. It is an estimate, not a census count.
LGA
Local government area. Lagos State has 20, and the heat gap is also reported within the LGAs that have enough residents in both groups.
Heat gap
How much warmer the land surface is where residents beyond 300 m from vegetation live than where residents within 300 m live.
Cross-sectional association
Two things that go together across places at one time. It does not show that one causes the other.
