# State machines: explorer and study page

Audit of 8 November 2026. The explorer was tested at d00e481 (unchanged since then). The study page (`docs/index.html`, `docs/assets/app.js`) was tested at a934ce5, a commit made by someone else during the audit. Testing used Playwright and Chromium against a local `http.server`. Line numbers are explore.html (E) or app.js (A) at those versions.

All of the explorer's state lives in one closure. `STATE` (E:1350) holds `features, summary, change, period, swipe, swipeX, lga, layer, tab, rankBy, compA, compB`. Module variables hold the rest: `basemapLayer`, `lgaLayer` (E:1380), `LAST_MAP` (E:1450), `overlays`, `shown` (E:1520-1521), `playTimer` (E:1566), `charts` (E:1802), and `tourIdx`, `tourReturn`, `tourLayer` (E:2112). Several states are also kept in the DOM: `aria-checked`, `hidden`, `open`, `checked`, `<select>.value`. None of it is reachable from the console.

**Race conditions tested that showed no defect.**
- Moving the slider while playing stops playback (E:1564).
- Fast period changes 2 → 1 → 0 → 1, with a 3 s-delayed image, ended with the correct image (2004–2013) and a matching legend. The overlay of a superseded period is removed from the map before it loads.
- Playing, then switching layer, stops the timer.
