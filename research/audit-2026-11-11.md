## Open: needs new analysis, not done here

- Own-cell greenness is not controlled. A reviewer's check outside the repository found the built-up gap falls sharply when cells of equal greenness are compared. That calculation is not in a repository script, so it is not quoted.
- No confidence interval. A spatial block bootstrap, crossed with resampling of passes, is feasible and not done.
- The gap is not reported per image or per month. A reviewer's check outside the repository found large differences between December and January or February images.
- The countryside reference was not varied, and the minimum patch size and the access distance were not varied.
- The headline 1.4 °C includes heat mixing between neighbouring cells; the gap was still falling at the widest buffer (120 m).
- Cells without an NDVI value were not diagnosed.
- No independent population layer was tried, and no comparison with ground temperature.
- The selection counts by path, and the 138-pass screening-off check, include passes that barely cover the state.

## Open: housekeeping

- Unused files remain until deletion is approved: `docs/assets/app.js`, `docs/data/overlays.json`, the old period maps in `docs/data/maps/`, `docs/vendor/`, `docs/assets/explorer-preview.png`.
- Typed numbers remain in `paper/template.md` (6,750 pixels, 5%, 3 °C, 4 K, 20 km, 10:03 UTC). They match the code constants today and nothing enforces it.
- The pipeline is not reproducible from the repository alone: the Planetary Computer query is live, the constrained population needs a manual download, and CI does not run the paper build.
- No tests cover the headline metrics or the Landsat processing.
- Normalised difference vegetation index values are not clipped to the valid range.
- Two references lack a DOI or year (USGS Landsat, WorldPop). The WorldCover accuracy figure in CONTRIBUTING.md (76.5%) was not verified.
- Phone map text is small, touch targets for the district shapes are under 44 px, and the page loads a font from a third-party service and a counter from another.
- The paper has six references and no related-work paragraph: nothing on Lagos, on how the surface heat island is defined, on the 300 m indicator or on spatial dependence.
